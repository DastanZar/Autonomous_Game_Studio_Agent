# Five experiments in one session (15 Oct 2026)

**The brief (user):** make as many films as possible, animated and non-animated, in about ten minutes, in a style unlike
anything we've made before, with full creative freedom.

Every frame and every sound is generated by code in `films.py`: no voice, no AI models, deterministic. Each film is
720×1280 (vertical) at 24 fps.

| Film | Kind | Idea | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pendulum.mp4` (30 s) | Physics animation | 15 pendulums; pendulum *k* swings 20+*k* times in 30 s, so they drift into waves and line up again at 30 s | Each bob plucks its own note of a pentatonic scale as it crosses the centre, so the rhythm you hear is the physics |
| `murmuration.mp4` (26 s) | Simulation | 1,400 starlings following three boids rules (separation, alignment, cohesion) over a dusk sky | Wind plus a slow drone |
| `ink.mp4` (24 s) | Generative art | 3,000 ink drops carried by a smooth, invisible current, leaving fading trails on paper | Three soft bell tones |
| `harmonograph.mp4` (24 s) | Generative art / maths | Two damped pendulums a fifth apart (3:2) draw a figure | The same two frequencies (220 and 330.6 Hz), decaying with the drawing |
| `earth.mp4` | **Non-animated:** real photographs | One day of NASA EPIC images of the whole sunlit Earth from DSCOVR, about 1.5 million km away, cross-dissolved in time order with the capture time in UTC | A low pad |

**Sources:**
- **Earth:** NASA EPIC (`epic.gsfc.nasa.gov`, public domain). The image list and timestamps are in `out/earth_sources.json`.
- **The physics films:** standard formulas only: pendulum period, Reynolds' boids, Lissajous curves.

**Not verified:** I can't hear the audio. Levels are normalised to a peak of 0.8; nothing was mixed by ear.

Rebuild any film with `python3 films.py <name>`.
