# Content plan: 21 Oct to 20 Nov 2026 (four Shorts channels)

Written 7 Oct 2026 by the content-strategist worker. It covers every slot from 21 Oct to 20 Nov **except** the
12 slots other workers are already filling: 21, 22, 27 and 28 Oct on Border Quirks, Gut Gang and Leader Flags. It
also plans the drawn channel's launch.

**How to read it.**
- Every slot has a working title, a hook, a series, the evidence for it and two candidate sources.
- Every hook is a **draft**. The research stage must fetch and quote a source for each number before scripting
  (house rule: facts beat jokes). A number marked *(verify)* hasn't been confirmed by me yet.
- "Estimate" labels a judgement of mine, not a measured figure.
- Our own YouTube Data API pulls are cited as **[API 7 Oct]**. Six searches were made: what happens when you get
  scared, daylight saving time, Berlin Wall, Statue of Liberty New Jersey, most Nobel prizes, Earth stopped spinning.
  The raw output was in the session scratchpad; the numbers needed are reproduced here.
  Cadence was measured with cheap playlist and video reads (no searches).
- The 6 Oct scout files are cited as **[scout]** (`docs/research/topics/*-2026-10-06.*`).

## 6. Cadence: posts per week, with the evidence

**Recommendation:** Border Quirks, Gut Gang and Leader Flags each post **4 a week** from the week of 19 Oct to
15 Nov. Step up to **5 a week (Monday to Friday) from 16 Nov** only if the 4-week review (Tue 10 Nov) passes the bar
in section 7. **Don't go daily this month,** even though the bible says "then 1/day" after the first two weeks.

**The evidence:**
1. **Per-Short performance peaks at 2–4 uploads a week, but monthly views keep rising with volume.** Accounts under
   10k subscribers saw the biggest Shorts gains: views up more than 200% year on year. Source: Metricool, 799,718
   videos from 71,177 accounts, 29 Jul 2026 ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/)). Four a week sits at
   the top of the efficient range. Five tests the volume upside once quality is proven.
2. **The niche benchmarks win on quality, not volume** [API 7 Oct, each channel's last 50 uploads]:

   | Channel | Shorts a week | Median views |
   |---|---|---|
   | GeoGlobeTales | 0.9 | **5.7M** |
   | SolarBalls | 0.8 | 0.88M |
   | Opera_cb | 0.6 | 0.44M |
   | Kurzgesagt | 1.2 | 1.3M |
   | Zack D. Films (the outlier, with a production team) | 34 | 8.5M |

   Their most common publishing hour is 14–15 UTC (10–11 ET). Zack's is 17–19 UTC. Our 15–18 UTC slots sit in or
   just after that window.
3. **Policy risk.** YouTube's "inauthentic content" update targets mass-produced, repetitive, TTS-narrated uploads
   ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/09/youtube-prepares-crackdown-on-mass-produced-and-repetitive-videos-as-concern-over-ai-slop-grows)).
   Three channels at 7 a week (21 Shorts a week, from one studio, with one synthetic voice) is exactly that pattern.
   Twelve to fifteen a week, each one sourced, is defensible.
4. **The data arrives slowly.** Shorts analytics needs about 48–72 hours to settle (estimate). At 4 a week, each
   review window has enough uploads (8+) to compare without flooding the feed with untested variants.
5. **Weekdays only.** Tuesday to Thursday carry the highest ad rates; weekends run 10–25% lower
   ([launch slate](research/launch-slate-2026-10.md)). Ad rates don't matter until the Partner Program, but the habit
   costs nothing. The exception is the drawn channel's Halloween Saturday.

## 8. Risks and what this plan doesn't know

- **Duplicates.** I checked every `episodes/*/*/topic.json` again at the end (7 Oct, late). Workers had claimed:
  - Border Quirks: Statue of Liberty, Dracula/Transylvania, Pheasant Island, Market Island (plus Swiss–Liechtenstein);
  - Gut Gang: cave-clock, cold-feet, contagious-yawn, sun-sneeze;
  - Leader Flags: pumpkins, MLB players, meat per person, nuclear weapons.

  I removed or replaced my overlapping picks: Statue of Liberty, Transylvania, pumpkins, military spending, turkey
  production, the DST body clock, cold day by day, yawning, holding in a sneeze. **Re-run the check before assigning
  any slot.**
- **Unverified hook numbers** are marked *(verify)*. Each needs a fetched, quoted source at the research stage, or
  it gets softened.
- **Manual uploads.** The API audit hasn't passed, so all 56 uploads in this plan are scheduled by hand in YouTube
  Studio (about 2–3 minutes each, estimate). Sixteen a week per person is the practical ceiling until the audit
  passes; that's another reason not to go daily.
- **Voice.** Every episode uses local Kokoro until an OpenRouter key exists. The drawn channel needs its voice chosen
  from the free catalogue before launch.
- **Sensitive slots,** to tell straight with no jokes about victims:
  - 11 Nov (Armistice deaths);
  - 16 Nov (colonial Africa);
  - 3 Nov (elections: mechanism and data only, no parties).
- **What I couldn't do:**
  - query TikTok or Instagram directly (no API; trend roundups only, and those were thin for Q3);
  - get YouTube's own figure for APV (none is published);
  - get Google Trends data for 2026;
  - confirm handle availability on TikTok and Instagram.
