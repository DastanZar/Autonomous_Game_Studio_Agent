# Hand-drawn format: which niche? (research, 7 Oct 2026)

Question from the user: the drawn-on-paper format is a blank canvas. Where should it go, based on data (last quarter
and more), crowding, and what a pencil does well?

## How this was measured

- **YouTube Data API**, our own key, 64 searches, about 6,500 quota units. Raw data: `yt-scan-2026-10-07.json`,
  `yt-scan2-2026-10-07.json`, `yt-trend-q2-q3-2026.json`.
  - For each query: the top 50 English videos by views in the window.
  - Measured per query:
    - median and top-10 views;
    - distinct channels;
    - "big channels" (1M+ subscribers);
    - "small breakouts" (a channel under 100k subscribers with a video over 500k views: the topic, not the audience,
      carried it).
- **Trend:** the same queries compared for Q2 (Apr–Jun) and Q3 (Jul–Sep) 2026.
  - Q3 videos have had less time to collect views, so about 0.5× is the neutral line.
  - Above it means the topic is growing; below it means it's cooling.
- **Wider internet:** TikTok and Reels trend roundups (Hootsuite, Later, Sprout, ContentGrip, Envato, Sept 2026),
  faceless-niche guides (vidIQ, OutlierKit, Shortimize, NexLev), and TikTok's own "draw my life" discovery pages.
  Reddit and X were reachable only through those roundups; I couldn't query them directly.

**3. Crowding.**
- **Science:** owned by no one except Zack D. Films (28.8M subscribers, 3D). The rest of the top 50 is full of tiny
  channels with 10–45M-view hits:
  - Under Six Minutes Studio (15.5k subscribers), 26M;
  - Mr Decode (60k), 17M;
  - Shockora (21k), 12M.

  Nearly all of it is 3D or AI-generated video. Nobody is drawing it by hand.
- **Economy and news:** owned by big outlets and exam-prep channels (StudyIQ, MS NOW, IAS channels). Hard for a new
  channel to break into. This is where the power-bill pilot sits.
- **History:** competitive but open (21 small breakouts). We already have Border Quirks in the history/geography lane.

## Our top three, best first

### 1. "What would actually happen if…": real science, drawn by hand (recommended)

- **Examples:** what would happen if you fell into Jupiter, if the Moon disappeared, if you stopped sleeping for a week,
  if every ant on Earth vanished. Each answer is worked out from real physics, biology or chemistry, with sources.
  It's xkcd's "What If?" idea (stick figures plus real science, a proven bestseller) as a Short.
- **Data:** the highest current median of any lane (6.0M), the best-holding big format, science surging ×4.9, and the
  most small-channel breakouts.
- **Synergy:** a hypothetical has no footage, so it has to be drawn.
  - Diagrams, arrows, scale comparisons and "here's you, here's Jupiter" are exactly what a pencil does best.
  - Deadpan jokes land in the margins.
  - No real people need to be drawn (our no-likeness rule).
- **Gap:** competitors are 3D or AI video. A hand-drawn, sourced version looks and feels different in the feed.
- **Risk:** "what if" without science is cooling (×0.36); the science answer is what keeps it alive. Zack D. Films is
  the giant. Our angle is the honest, worked answer, not shock.

### 2. History as a story, through one lens

- **Example:** "a day in the life of…" (a Roman legionary, a medieval peasant, a pirate crew member), what it cost, what
  they ate, how they died.
- **Data:** 2.9–3.2M median and 21 small breakouts, holding steady. Experts' advice for history is "one lens applied
  across eras", so every episode sells the next.
- **Synergy:** "draw my life", done for ordinary people of the past, uses the trend's own grammar with no real-person
  likeness.
- **Risk:** it overlaps Border Quirks' history audience. It would need to be people and daily life, never maps.

### 3. The money behind everyday things (the pilot's lane)

- **Example:** why your power bill, rent or groceries went up, drawn.
- **Data:** steady (×1.07) but a 0.26–0.34M median, and dominated by big outlets. Lowest reach of the three, though
  finance pays the highest ad rates.
- **Synergy:** good. Charts and arrows suit the pencil, and SportsBall-style dense pages fit it.
- **Verdict:** keep it as an occasional episode, not the channel's identity.

**Recommendation:** make the channel "What would actually happen if…", drawn. The power-bill pilot stays as the format
test. The next pilot is a science "what if" so we can compare.
