studio-publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

studio-publisher is a private, internal tool. It uploads videos that its owner made to the owner's own YouTube channels: Border Quirks, Gut Gang and Leader Flags. It is not offered to anyone else, and it has no users other than the owner.

What it accesses

  • Through the YouTube Data API, with the owner's permission, it can upload videos and caption tracks to the owner's channels (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl).
  • It doesn't read, collect or store any other Google user data, viewer data, comments or analytics about other people.

What it stores

  • One OAuth refresh token per channel, kept as a private environment secret in the owner's own build environment. Tokens are never published or shared.
  • A record of each upload (video ID, link and time) in the owner's project files.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
