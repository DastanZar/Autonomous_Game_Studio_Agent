## Decided / found / skipped
- Licence grant is "Solely within the Applicable Territory" (excluding the US, EU, UK, Korea); publishing outputs to viewers there is unresolved.
  Recommended: skip H3 for published work unless MiniMax confirms in writing; use Wan 2.2 and LTX.
- Size: 33.1B dense. Our arithmetic: 4-bit is about 17–19 GB, so it won't fit in 16 GB RAM; Wan2GP's "5–6 GB VRAM" means offloading to system RAM.
  The RAM requirement isn't published (not found).
- UGC ads: fake AI customers are banned in the US by the FTC rule; not recommended. A labelled AI presenter is a separate idea, out of scope.
