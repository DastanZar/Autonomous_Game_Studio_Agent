# 2026-10-15 question: 10-minute videos with real footage, or stick to Shorts?

## User message (verbatim)
is it possible to be making 10 minute yt videos, you just by yourself, but apart from very sleect frames nothing is code generated but actual footage and animations and illustrations? what sort of such videos are doing the best views these days, are they news, are they something else? or is it better to stick to making shorts

## Decided / found / skipped
- Answer: yes, it's feasible. The limit is footage rights, not craft. Sources: public-domain archive (NASA, NARA, LoC,
  Prelinger, Wikimedia), free or paid stock, AI reconstructions (needs credit or the laptop), and image-model illustrations
  with 2.5D moves. Code is kept for maps, text, diagrams and data.
- Munich 1972 already proved the documentary look; it can't be published because of AP/Getty rights.
- Recommendation: keep the Shorts channels and add one long-form documentary channel in a niche rich in public-domain
  footage (20th-century US history, the Cold War, the space race, disasters), one video every 1–2 weeks, with 3–4 Shorts
  cut from each. News: no (short shelf life, broadcaster-owned footage, daily fact-checking cost); "the backstory of this week's
  news" is the better angle.
- Long-form performance claims are not freshly measured; offered a yt-dlp scan of long-form medians before picking a niche.
