# Hook patterns

Evidence: the top Shorts of the benchmark channels (docs/research/shorts-niches-2026-09.md, measured
2026-09-28). Update this file from `performance.jsonl` once our own data exists (see sop/10).

| Pattern | Shape | Evidence (views) |
|---|---|---|
| The absurd true event | "In <year>, <actor> <did absurd thing>." + one-word punch | Our Emu War hook; HAI "How Blink 182 violated the Geneva Conventions" (3.2M) |
| Why-question with the fact inside | "Why wasn't Portugal conquered by Spain?" | Knowledgia (10M) |
| Things that existed | "<Impossible thing> actually existed" | Mustard "Flying Aircraft Carriers Actually Existed" (4.8M) |
| Matchup | "<A> vs <B>" with an unfair twist | SolarBalls "A Commercial Plane vs the Solar System" (10M) |
| What happens if / how to survive | "What to do if <danger>" | Zack D. Films "What To Do If Your Car Sinks?" (9.9M) |
| Hidden place / body | "What rains on each planet?" "Inside of Big Ben" | SolarBalls (3.6M); Jared Owen (2.3M) |
| You believe the wrong thing | "The Sun isn't the center of the solar system" | Cleo Abram (7.5M) |

Rules of thumb (to be confirmed by our own data):
- The surprise is in the sentence, not promised by it.
- Under 12 words. Proper nouns and a number beat adjectives.
- A question hook must be answerable in the video, and the answer must be the payoff.
